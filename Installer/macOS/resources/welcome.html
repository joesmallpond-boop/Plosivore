Plosivore __VERSION__

Removes plosives and mic pops from vocals, and shows you each one it catches as a pink bubble.

This installs:

VST3 pluginfor Ableton Live, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper and Bitwig
Audio Unitfor Logic Pro and GarageBand
Standalone appto try it without a DAW

To install only some of these, click Customize on the Installation Type step.

Quit your DAW before continuing.
